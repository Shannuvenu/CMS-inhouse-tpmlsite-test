CELEBRATING WORLD HEART DAY WITH MANIPAL

DH Brandspot conceptualized and executed a comprehensive, week-long campaign for Manipal Hospital to commemorate World Heart Day. This multi-faceted initiative comprised a range of engaging activities, including:

- CPR Lifesaver training sessions, empowering individuals with life-saving skills
- Heartathon, a fitness and health engagement program promoting cardiovascular wellness
- A dedicated program facilitating emergency ambulance services, ensuring timely medical intervention - A social media awareness drive leveraging heart-shaped red lights, sparking conversations and promoting heart health

These interconnected activations created immersive engagement platforms for clients, patients, and the broader community. By fostering education, health awareness, and community outreach, Manipal Hospital reaffirmed its commitment to prioritizing heart health and wellness.

AMFI – Investor Awareness Programmes

The Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) teamed up with Deccan Herald and Prajavani to bring the 'Bharat Nivesh Yatra' to 12 key cities across the state of Karnataka.

AMFI, a leading body representing mutual funds in the country, has been raising awareness about the importance of mutual fund investments across India.

More than 800 attendees had the opportunity to engage with experts and learn the fundamentals of mutual fund investment, empowering them to make informed financial decisions.
